Who I am

I am a mechatronics and robotics-focused Junior Student at VinUniversity, Vietnam. I'm interested in building systems that combine sensing, actuation, control, and real-world deployment. I work in multiple zone of robotic including autonomous UAVs, soft robotic mechanisms, and human-centered robotic design. My projects often involve turning research concepts into working prototypes by combining hardware design, system integration, and testing. I enjoy working across the full development process, from concept generation and CAD design to sensor integration, controller implementation, and experimental evaluation. This includes tasks such as developing soft actuation systems, integrating embedded electronics, building robotic platforms, and refining system performance through iterative testing and data analysis. I am particularly drawn to projects that require system-level thinking, where mechanical, electrical, and software components must work together reliably. Beyond technical implementation, I am also interested in the research side of robotics, especially in areas where innovative design can create meaningful real-world impact. I enjoy exploring how robotics can be applied to autonomous systems, assistive technologies, and bio-inspired or soft robotic devices. My goal is to continue developing as an engineer and researcher who can bridge theory and practice, translating ideas from the lab into robust and useful robotic solutions.

I enjoy translating research ideas into working prototypes, especially in projects that require a mix of mechanical design, embedded integration, system-level thinking, and experimental validation.

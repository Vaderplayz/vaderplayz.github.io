A Soft-Actuator-Integrated Thrombectomy Catheter for High-Speed Distal Rotation and Controlled Localized Axial Elongation

First-author research on a soft-actuator-integrated thrombectomy catheter designed for high-speed distal rotation and controlled localized axial elongation.

Research status: Submitted to ICRA 2027. Only approved public information is shown while the manuscript is under review.

Project Overview

The project investigates a catheter-scale soft-actuation approach that combines distal rotation with controlled localized axial elongation for mechanical thrombectomy.

  • First-author research contribution.
  • Soft-actuator integration for catheter-scale motion.
  • High-speed distal rotation and controlled localized axial elongation.
  • Submitted to ICRA 2027.

Flagship UAV Project

Dual-2D-LiDAR UAV for Indoor SLAM, 3D Reconstruction, and Precision Landing

One project developed through two stages: simulation validation and real-world deployment. The same system architecture connects PX4, ROS 2, LiDAR mapping, point-cloud reconstruction, and AprilTag-based precision landing in GPS-denied environments.

Panel 1

Simulation and Pipeline Validation

  • Validated the architecture using PX4 SITL, ROS 2, and RViz.
  • Tested lawnmower, spiral, and diagonal survey trajectories.
  • Used a horizontal virtual LiDAR for 2D SLAM and a vertical virtual LiDAR for sequential scans.
  • Stitched scans with UAV pose estimates to generate a 3D point cloud.
  • Used the simulation stage to debug coordinate frames, scan orientation, pose synchronization, and map generation before hardware deployment.

Panel 2

Real-World Deployment

  • Built and integrated a custom 10-inch PX4 UAV with Raspberry Pi 5.
  • Deployed horizontal-LiDAR indoor SLAM on the physical platform.
  • Integrated a second vertical LiDAR for real environmental scanning and point-cloud reconstruction.
  • Tested AprilTag detection, local-pose estimation, and precision landing without GPS.
  • Performed hardware debugging, flight testing, power-system troubleshooting, ROS 2 integration, and map-quality evaluation.
  • Vertical-LiDAR reconstruction remains under active improvement and is presented as ongoing work.
